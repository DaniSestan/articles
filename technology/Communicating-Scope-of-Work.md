# Communicating Scope of Work

When someone's opinion of any code repository they've worked on is "I don't want to know to resolve the issues 
I approved and/or authored", it leads to just one requirement for an individual tasked with refactoring their work:
Consider it time to lean back in some office chair taking a "fake" vacation. This is the easiest option for 
communicating, "I haven't figured out how to resolve some of my own problems, let alone yours; Therefore, it doesn't 
need to be a problem. I'm just here to enjoy the view now. I like what they've done with the place. Is that a new 
office plant? Ah, it's the same one. Must be a new pot. Great glazing."
